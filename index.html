    Philosophy

    History is made by
    a handful of people.

    Rome, Florence, Los Alamos, Starbase. Every era’s turning point traces back to a few individuals with unusual conviction. The centuries change; the people don’t. Our work is to recognise them early, and to stand beside them for the long run.

    1. People before markets.

      We back the person first. Great founders find the market, reshape it, or create one that did not exist.

    2. The earliest conviction.

      We invest before the evidence is obvious, when belief matters most and capital is hardest to find.

    3. Consequential ideas.

      We look for work that changes what is possible, across AI, science, infrastructure and the frontiers that come after them.

    4. Long horizons.

      History is written over decades. We build relationships to match: candid, patient, and in it for what comes next.
